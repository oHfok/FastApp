Settings

Dashboard Theme

More than a color swap — typography, shape, and density change too. Applies instantly, saved on this device only.

Timeline Colors

"Individual App" gives every app its own color, so a busy day's Timeline ribbon is actually readable at a glance instead of a few apps sharing categories blurring together. "By Category" colors by category instead, like the rest of the dashboard.

Timeline Range

"Full Day" always spans midnight to midnight. "Fit to Activity" trims the axis to your first and last recorded session — on a typical day most of the 24 hours are asleep or away, so trimming makes the hours you actually used several times wider on screen. Falls back to the full day when a day already spans most of it.

Database Retention

How long detailed session and macro logs are kept. Anything older is permanently deleted the next time FastApp starts, and cannot be recovered without a backup. Your daily totals are never affected.

Backup & Restore

Download a complete copy of your tracked history as a single file. Everything lives in one file on this PC — worth keeping a copy of that somewhere else too.

Download Backup

Restoring replaces everything currently tracked with the backup file's contents, and restarts FastApp. Your current data is saved first, just in case — but this isn't something to click through by accident.

Saved.
Window Title Capture

Off by default. When on, the Activity feed also records each window's title (e.g. a browser tab's page name or a document's filename) alongside the app name — so you can tell "coding in VS Code" apart from "watching YouTube in Chrome." Titles can contain anything the page/document author put there, so treat this as more sensitive than plain app-usage tracking.
